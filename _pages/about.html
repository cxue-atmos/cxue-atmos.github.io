ATMOSPHERIC CHEMISTRY · SURFACE–ATMOSPHERE EXCHANGE

Understanding the exchanges
that shape our atmosphere.

I am a Professor at the Institute of Atmospheric Physics, Chinese Academy of Sciences. My research explores how trace gases are exchanged between Earth’s surface and the atmosphere, and how these processes influence atmospheric chemistry, air quality, and climate.

My group investigates reactive nitrogen, greenhouse gases, and their interactions across terrestrial and marine environments. We combine field observations, laboratory experiments, instrument development, and atmospheric modeling with artificial intelligence and machine learning (AI/ML) to understand the processes connecting surface exchange, atmospheric composition, and environmental change.
